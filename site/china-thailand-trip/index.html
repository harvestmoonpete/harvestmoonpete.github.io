THAILAND

Jan 8–14

JAN 8

Lijiang → Kunming → Bangkok

Working option: morning Lijiang → Kunming, then TG613 to Bangkok. Settle in around 6:30–7:30 PM and keep the evening easy.

JAN 9

Grand Palace + Wat Pho + Wat Arun + Yaowarat

Early Grand Palace, Wat Pho, river ferry to Wat Arun, slow afternoon, then Chinatown from sunset into the evening.

JAN 10

Bangkok → Pattaya + beach / sunset

Private car around 10:30–11:00 AM. Arrive about 12:30–1:30 PM, settle in, relax by the beach, then Khao Phra Tamnak viewpoint for sunset.

JAN 11

Sanctuary of Truth + Pattaya evening

Morning at the Sanctuary of Truth, lunch around Naklua, afternoon hotel break, then beachfront / Central Pattaya / optional Walking Street.

JAN 12

Koh Larn island day

Morning ferry or speedboat from Bali Hai Pier, spend the day at one or two beaches, then return to Pattaya before evening.

JAN 13

Nong Nooch + Buddha Mountain

Morning at Nong Nooch Tropical Garden, continue to Khao Chi Chan / Buddha Mountain, then return for a relaxed final Pattaya night.

JAN 14

Pattaya → Bangkok Airport → Chongqing

Private transfer from Pattaya around 2:30–3:00 PM, then working flight 3U8188 around 7:20 PM BKK → 11:30 PM CKG.
